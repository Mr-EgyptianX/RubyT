RubyT is a lightweight and customizable AI-focused browser that brings a wide range of AI tools and online services together in one simple application. The app organizes AI services into convenient categories such as Chat, Images, Programming, Video, Audio, Search, Writing, and Social. RubyT provides quick access to popular AI platforms and tools, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Copilot, Grok, Perplexity, and many others. The app is designed to make accessing AI services easier, especially on devices where dedicated AI applications may not be available or practical. RubyT also allows users to add their own websites and create custom sections. These custom sections and websites are private to the user. Additional security systems will be added in future versions to help protect user data and privacy. Known Technical Issue: There is currently a technical issue with TikTok in the Social section. TikTok may open in the Chrome browser instead of inside RubyT. This issue is being addressed and is planned to be improved in future releases. Key Features: - Multiple AI categories in one application - Quick access to popular AI services - Custom websites and sections - Private user customization - Lightweight design - Data-saving features - Simple and organized interface - Convenient access to AI tools from one place.Another very important piece of information: Firstly, the application is in its experimental phase, meaning it currently contains many errors.
